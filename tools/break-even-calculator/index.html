Break-Even Point Calculator

Every sale contributes price minus variable cost toward your fixed costs. Find the point where you stop losing money.

How it's calculated

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit).

Questions

What are fixed and variable costs?

Fixed costs (rent, software) stay the same however much you sell; variable costs (materials, packaging) rise with each unit.
