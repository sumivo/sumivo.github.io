Flooring & Tile Calculator

Add a waste allowance for cuts. Around 10% for straight layouts and 15% or more for diagonal or patterned ones.

How it's calculated

Material = length × width × (1 + waste %); boxes = material ÷ coverage per box, rounded up.

Questions

Should I keep spare boxes?

A box or two from the same batch makes future repairs much easier to match.
