How it's calculated

Price = cost ÷ (1 − margin). A 40% margin needs a 66.7% markup.

Questions

Why isn't it cost × 1.4 for a 40% margin?

That gives a 40% markup, which is only a 28.6% margin. Margin is measured on the selling price.
