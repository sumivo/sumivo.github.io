Selling Price from Target Margin Calculator

Know your cost and the margin you want? Here is the price to charge. It's more than cost plus the margin percentage.

How it's calculated

Price = cost ÷ (1 − margin). A 40% margin needs a 66.7% markup.

Questions

Why isn't it cost × 1.4 for a 40% margin?

That gives a 40% markup, which is only a 28.6% margin. Margin is measured on the selling price.
