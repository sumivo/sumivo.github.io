Customer Acquisition Cost Payback Calculator

For subscriptions and repeat buyers, payback time shows how quickly marketing spend comes back.

How it's calculated

CAC = spend ÷ new customers. Payback = CAC ÷ (monthly revenue × gross margin).

Worked example

InputValue
Marketing spend6,000
New customers won20
Revenue per customer / month40
Gross margin %75
ResultValue

Payback period10 months
