Questions

Why double the cost for wholesale and retail?

The 2x/2x rule leaves room for profit, shop fees and future wholesale deals. Change the markup if your market is different.

What fees should I enter?

Add up your marketplace's transaction fee and the payment processing percentage. Check your platform's current fee page, as fees change.
