Party Food & Drink Quantity Calculator

These are common rule-of-thumb amounts for a drinks-and-nibbles party. Adjust them for your crowd.

How it's calculated

Appetizers = guests × (first-hour amount + later-hour amount × extra hours). Ice ≈ 1.5 lb per guest.

Worked example

InputValue
Guests30
Party length (hours)3
Appetizers per guest in first hour6
Appetizers per guest each later hour4
Drinks per guest per hour1
ResultValue
Appetizer pieces420
Drinks90

Questions

Is this a full meal?

No. If you're serving a meal, plan fewer appetizers, about half.
