Recipe Scaler (Servings Converter)

Enter the original servings, the servings you want and up to four ingredient amounts in any unit.

How it's calculated

New amount = original amount × (servings wanted ÷ original servings).

Questions

Does baking time change?

Often. Bigger batches in deeper pans need longer; check doneness rather than the clock.
