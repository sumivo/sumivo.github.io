Rent vs Buy Calculator (First-Year Cost)

Buying builds equity, but owning has costs rent doesn't. This compares the unrecoverable costs of each for the first year.

How it's calculated

Owning cost ≈ mortgage interest + tax + upkeep + insurance + lost returns on the down payment − appreciation. Principal repaid isn't a cost, because it becomes equity.

Questions

Why compare unrecoverable costs?

Rent, interest, tax and upkeep are money you don't get back. Comparing those is a fairer test than rent against the full mortgage payment.
