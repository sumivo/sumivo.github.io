Working Capital & Current Ratio Calculator

These ratios show whether short-term assets cover short-term debts.

How it's calculated

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities. Quick ratio excludes inventory.

Worked example

InputValue
Current assets85,000
Current liabilities50,000
Inventory (included in assets)20,000
ResultValue

Current ratio1.7
Quick ratio1.3

Questions

What is a healthy current ratio?

Often between about 1.2 and 2; below 1 means short-term debts exceed short-term assets.
