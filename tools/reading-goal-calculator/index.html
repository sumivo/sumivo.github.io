Reading Goal Calculator (Books per Year)

Want to read 24 books this year? Here's what that means each day.

How it's calculated

Pages per day = books × pages ÷ days; minutes per day = pages per day ÷ pages per hour × 60.

Worked example

InputValue
Books goal24
Average pages per book300
Days left to reach it365
Pages you read per hour40
ResultValue
Pages per day19.73
Minutes per day30
Days per book15.21
