Case Converter (Upper, Lower, Title & Sentence Case)

Fix text typed with Caps Lock on, format headings in Title Case, or turn phrases into camelCase and snake_case for code. Paste your text, click a conversion and copy the result; Undo takes you back.

How to change text case

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Click a conversion such as Sentence case or Title Case.
  3. Copy the result or download it. Press Undo to go back a step.

Proper Title Case

Title Case follows common English style rules, keeping short words such as “a”, “of” and “the” in lowercase unless they start the title or follow a colon.

Clean-up tools

Remove extra spaces, line breaks or empty lines from text copied out of PDFs and emails, so it pastes neatly into documents and forms.

Questions

How do I fix text typed in all caps?

Click Sentence case, which lowercases everything and capitalises the start of each sentence.

Does it work with other languages?

Yes, for scripts that have upper and lower case (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek). Scripts without case, such as Hindi, are left unchanged.

Is my text stored?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
