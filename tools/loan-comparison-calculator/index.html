Loan Comparison Calculator

A lower rate with a long term can cost more than a higher rate with a short one. Compare the real totals.

How it's calculated

Each loan's cost = payment × number of payments − amount borrowed + fees.

Worked example

InputValue
Amount borrowed20,000
Loan A APR %7
Loan A term (years)5
Loan A fees300
Loan B APR %8.5
Loan B term (years)3
Loan B fees0
ResultValue
Loan A monthly payment$396.02
Loan A total cost (interest + fees)$4,061

Loan B total cost (interest + fees)$2,729
