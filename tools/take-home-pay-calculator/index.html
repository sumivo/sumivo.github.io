How it's calculated

Net ≈ gross × (1 − retirement %) × (1 − income taxes) − gross × payroll %. A simplified estimate using flat rates.

Questions

Why is this only an estimate?

Real taxes use brackets, allowances and credits. Use your effective rates from a recent payslip for the best estimate.

Results are estimates for planning and education, not financial, tax or legal advice.
