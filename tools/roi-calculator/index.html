ROI Calculator (Return on Investment)

ROI compares what you gained with what you put in. It is useful for investments, equipment, courses and side projects.

How it's calculated

ROI = (final value − cost − fees) ÷ (cost + fees).

Worked example

InputValue
Amount invested5,000
Final value or total return7,200
Fees & extra costs50
ResultValue
Net profit$2,150.00
ROI42.6%

Questions

Why doesn't ROI show time?

ROI ignores how long it took. Use the CAGR calculator to compare investments held for different periods.
