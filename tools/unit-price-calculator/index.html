Unit Price Comparison Calculator

Bigger isn't always cheaper. Enter the price and quantity of two options.

How it's calculated

Unit price = price ÷ quantity. Savings = 1 − cheaper unit price ÷ dearer unit price.

Worked example

InputValue
Option A price4.99
Option A quantity (oz, g, items)16
Option B price7.49
Option B quantity28
ResultValue
