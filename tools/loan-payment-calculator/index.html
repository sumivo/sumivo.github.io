Loan Payment Calculator

Enter the amount you want to borrow, the interest rate and the term to see your fixed monthly payment and what the loan really costs.

How it's calculated

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where P is the amount borrowed, r the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12) and n the number of monthly payments.

Questions

Does a longer term save money?

It lowers the monthly payment but almost always increases the total interest you pay. Compare both numbers before choosing.
