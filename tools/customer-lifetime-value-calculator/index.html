Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Calculator

Knowing what a customer is worth over time tells you how much you can afford to spend to win one.

How it's calculated

CLV = order value × purchases per year × years × margin.

Worked example

Purchases per year4

Questions

What is the 3:1 rule?

A common guideline that lifetime value should be at least three times what it costs to acquire a customer.
