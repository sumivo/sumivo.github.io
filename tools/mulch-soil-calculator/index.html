Mulch, Soil & Gravel Calculator

Enter the bed size and depth. Bulk suppliers sell by the cubic yard; garden centres by the bag.

How it's calculated

Cubic feet = length × width × depth (inches) ÷ 12; cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27.

Questions

How deep should mulch be?

Around 2–3 inches is typical for beds. Keep it away from plant stems and tree trunks.
