Text to Speech (Read Aloud)

Listen to articles, notes, emails or study material instead of reading them. Paste the text, pick a voice and speed, and press Read aloud; the sentence being read is highlighted so you can follow along.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Files stay on your device
  • Works on phone and computer
Advertisement

How to use text to speech

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Choose a voice (voices depend on your device and browser), and set the speed and pitch.
  3. Press Read aloud. Use Pause and Stop at any time.

Voices on your device

The tool uses the speech voices built into your operating system and browser, including many high-quality natural voices in Edge and Chrome. More languages can be added in your system's speech or language settings.

Great for learning and accessibility

Hearing text helps with proofreading, language learning, dyslexia and tired eyes. Slow the speed down to follow along or speed it up to get through long documents.

Questions

Is text to speech free?

Yes, with no limits.

Can I download the audio as MP3?

Not at the moment. Browsers don't allow saving the built-in voices as files, so this tool reads aloud only.

Why are there different voices on my phone and computer?

Voices come from your device. Install additional voices in your system's speech settings.
