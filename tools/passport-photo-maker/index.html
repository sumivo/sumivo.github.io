Passport Photo Maker (India, US, UK & More)

Turn a phone photo into a passport-size photo. The tool finds your face, straightens a tilted head, replaces the background with plain white (or another allowed colour) and sizes the head from crown to chin for the document you choose. It also checks common rules such as eyes open, facing the camera and a neutral expression. Download a single photo for online forms or a print sheet for a photo shop. Your photo never leaves your device.

How to make a passport size photo

  1. Choose the photo type, for example India passport (35×45 mm) or US passport (2×2 in).
  2. Choose a photo of your face, taken straight on in good light.
  3. Read the photo checks, and fine-tune if needed by dragging, zooming or rotating so the top of the head and the chin sit on the dashed lines.
  4. Download the single photo (set a maximum size in KB for online forms) or a 4×6 in print sheet.

Automatic face positioning

Google's open-source MediaPipe face landmark model finds the chin, eyes and nose on your device. The top of the head is taken from the cut-out above your face, the eyes are levelled, and the photo is scaled so the crown-to-chin height matches the chosen document. If no face can be found, the position is estimated from your outline and you can adjust it.

Sizes included

India passport 35×45 mm, India visa and OCI 2×2 in, PAN card 25×35 mm, US passport and visa 2×2 in, UK passport 35×45 mm, Schengen visa 35×45 mm, Canada 50×70 mm, Australia 35×45 mm, China visa 33×48 mm, or any custom size. Photos are made at 300 dpi, the standard print resolution.

Printing at a photo shop

The print sheet places as many photos as fit on 4×6 in (10×15 cm) photo paper, 5×7 in or A4, with thin cut lines. Ask the shop to print it at actual size, not “fit to page”. A 4×6 print usually costs a few rupees and gives six 35×45 mm photos.

Getting an accepted photo

Face the camera with a neutral expression, mouth closed and both eyes open; no glasses, hats or filters; even lighting without shadows; hair away from the face. The tool flags tilted or turned heads, closed eyes, smiles and low resolution, but it can't check everything (glasses, shadows, lighting), and rules differ between countries, so check the official requirements before you submit. We can't guarantee acceptance.

Questions

What size is an Indian passport photo?

35×45 mm (3.5×4.5 cm) with a white background, the face covering about 80% of the photo. Choose “India passport” and the tool sizes it for you.

Can I change the background to white?

Yes. The background is removed automatically and replaced with white, off-white, light grey or light blue.

My head is slightly tilted in the photo. Can it be fixed?

Yes. Tilts of up to 15 degrees are straightened automatically, and you can fine-tune with the Rotate slider.

How do I get the photo under 50 KB or 100 KB for an online form?

Enter the limit in “Max file size” before downloading the photo.

How many photos fit on a 4×6 sheet?

Six 35×45 mm photos fit on a 4×6 in sheet with cutting space. For 2×2 in photos, 2 fit on 4×6 in paper and 6 on 5×7 in paper.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Face detection, background removal and cropping all happen in your browser.
