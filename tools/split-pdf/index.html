Split PDF (Extract Pages)

Pull pages out of a PDF or cut it into several smaller PDFs. Split every page into its own file, split by ranges such as 1-3, 4-10, or extract chosen pages into one new PDF. Several files download together as a ZIP.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF or drag it onto the box.
  2. Pick what to do - every page separately, split into ranges, or extract pages into one PDF.
  3. Type the pages or ranges, for example 1-3, 5, 8- (8 to the end).
  4. Press Split PDF.

Ranges made simple

Separate ranges with commas. “1-3, 4-6” makes two PDFs; “2, 5, 9” extracts three pages; “8-” means page 8 to the end. In extract mode all chosen pages go into one PDF, in the order you type them.

No quality loss

Pages are copied exactly with pdf-lib, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

Questions

Can I extract just one page from a PDF?

Yes. Choose “Extract pages into one PDF” and type the page number.

How do I get every page as a separate file?

Choose “Every page as a separate PDF”. The pages download together as a ZIP.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is split on your device.
