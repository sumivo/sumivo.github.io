Inventory Turnover Calculator

Turnover shows how quickly stock sells. Slow-moving stock ties up cash.

How it's calculated

Turnover = COGS ÷ average inventory. Days = 365 ÷ turnover.

Worked example

InputValue
Cost of goods sold (year)120,000
Inventory at start of year25,000
Inventory at end of year15,000
ResultValue
Average inventory$20,000
Turnover per year6.0
Days of inventory61 days
