QR Code Generator (Links, Wi-Fi, UPI)

Create a QR code for your website, menu, Wi-Fi network, UPI payments, business card and more. Choose the type, fill in the details and download a high-resolution PNG or a vector SVG for printing. These are static QR codes: they never expire and don't depend on any service staying online.

How to make a QR code

  1. Choose what the QR code is for - a link, Wi-Fi, UPI payment, contact card and so on.
  2. Fill in the details. The preview updates as you type.
  3. Optionally change the colours and error correction.
  4. Download it as PNG or SVG and test it with your phone camera before printing.

UPI payment QR codes

Enter your UPI ID, name and optionally a fixed amount and note to make a QR code that any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) can scan to pay you. Great for shops, stalls, tuition fees and splitting bills.

Wi-Fi QR codes

Guests scan the code to join your Wi-Fi without typing the password. Print it for your home, office, café or Airbnb.

No expiry, no tracking

Many “free” QR generators route scans through their own short links and stop working later unless you pay. These codes contain your information directly, so they keep working forever.

Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. They are static codes with your data inside, not links through our servers.

Can I make a QR code for UPI payments?

Yes. Choose “UPI payment”, enter your UPI ID and name, and optionally an amount.

Which format is best for printing?

SVG scales to any size without blurring; PNG at 2048 px is also fine for most prints.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Keep the code dark on a light background, or some phones won't scan it.
