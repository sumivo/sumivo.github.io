Image Resizer (Pixels, Percent & Presets)

Make images exactly the size you need. Enter a width and height, scale by a percentage, or choose a preset such as a 35×45 mm passport photo, an exam-form photo and signature, an Instagram post or a YouTube thumbnail. Batch resize in your browser.

How to resize an image

  1. Choose your images or drag them onto the box.
  2. Pick a preset, or enter a width and/or height in pixels, or a percentage.
  3. When setting both sides, choose to fit inside, crop to fill or stretch. For crops, pick smart crop or a position, or press “Adjust crop” to drag the crop box.
  4. Press Resize and download.

Presets for everyday needs

Presets cover passport and visa photos, exam-form photos and signatures, Instagram posts and stories, YouTube thumbnails, Facebook and X covers, profile pictures and HD wallpapers. Fixed-size presets crop rather than stretch. Smart crop keeps the most detailed part of each image (usually the subject), and you can drag the crop box for any image with “Adjust crop”.

Keep proportions

Enter only a width or only a height and the other side is calculated automatically, so your image never looks squashed.

Questions

How do I resize a photo for a passport or form?

Choose the matching preset. The photo is cropped to the exact size (use “Adjust crop” to frame the face, or the Passport photo maker for automatic face positioning); check your form's own rules for size in KB, and use the image compressor if needed.

Can I resize many images at once?

Yes. They download together as a ZIP.

Does resizing upload my images?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
