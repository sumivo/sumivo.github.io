Retirement Savings Calculator

See how big your nest egg could be at retirement, what that is worth after inflation, and the yearly income it might support.

How it's calculated

Future value = S(1 + r)^n + C × ((1 + r)^n − 1) ÷ r with monthly rate r; today's value divides by (1 + inflation)^years.

Questions

What is the 4% rule?

A rule of thumb that withdrawing about 4% of savings in the first year, then adjusting for inflation, has historically lasted around 30 years. It is not guaranteed.
