Mortgage Payment Calculator (with Tax & Insurance)

A mortgage payment is more than principal and interest. Add property tax, home insurance and HOA dues to see the number you will actually pay each month.

How it's calculated

Principal and interest use the standard amortization formula on the loan amount (price minus down payment). Tax and insurance are spread evenly over 12 months.

Worked example

InputValue
Home price400,000
Down payment %20
Interest rate %6.5
Term (years)30
Property tax % per year1.1
Home insurance per year1,500
HOA per month0
