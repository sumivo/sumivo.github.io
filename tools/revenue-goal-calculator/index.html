Revenue Goal Calculator (Sales & Visitors Needed)

Turn a vague goal into concrete numbers, the sales per month and the traffic needed to get them.

How it's calculated

Sales = profit goal ÷ (price × margin). Visitors = sales ÷ conversion rate.

Worked example

InputValue
Monthly profit goal3,000
Average price25
Profit margin %70
Conversion rate %2
ResultValue

Sales per day5.71
Visitors needed per month8,572

Questions

The visitor number is huge. Now what?

Raise the price or order value, improve conversion, or add products. Each cuts the traffic needed.
