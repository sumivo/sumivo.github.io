Trip Fuel Cost Calculator

Enter the trip distance, your car's fuel economy and the fuel price to budget a road trip or split costs fairly.

How it's calculated

Fuel = distance ÷ fuel economy; cost = fuel × price. Works the same with kilometres and litres if you keep units consistent.

Questions

Does speed matter?

Yes. Fuel economy usually drops at high motorway speeds and in heavy traffic.
