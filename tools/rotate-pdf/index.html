Rotate & Organize PDF Pages

Fix sideways scans, remove blank or unwanted pages and put pages in the right order. You see a thumbnail of every page, make your changes and save a new PDF. Pages are copied, not re-rendered, so nothing loses quality.

How to rotate or rearrange PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF or drag it onto the box.
  2. Use the buttons under each thumbnail to rotate, move or delete pages, or rotate all pages at once.
  3. Press Save PDF to download the edited file.

Permanent rotation

Rotation is saved into the PDF, so the pages stay the right way up in every PDF reader, on phones and when printed.

Private and lossless

The PDF is edited on your device with pdf-lib. Text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

Questions

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Use the rotate buttons under that page's thumbnail.

Can I delete pages from a PDF?

Yes. Press the ✕ under any page, then save.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes, it works with any PDF that isn't password-protected.
