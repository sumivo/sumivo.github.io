Translator (Free & Private, Works Offline)

Translate messages, emails and documents without sending your text to a translation company. The tool uses your browser's built-in on-device translator where available, and otherwise downloads an open-source translation model once and runs it on your device.

How to translate text

  1. Choose the language you're translating from and to.
  2. Type or paste up to 5,000 characters.
  3. Press Translate. The first time, the language model downloads; after that it's fast.

Private translation

Your text is translated on your device. Recent Chrome versions on computers include an on-device translator that we use when it supports your languages. Otherwise the open-source Opus-MT models (about 115 MB per language pair) or the M2M100 model (about 650 MB, 100 languages) download once and run in your browser.

Indian and world languages

Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu and Nepali, plus Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic and more. Pairs without a direct model are translated through English.

Check important translations

Machine translation is great for understanding and drafting, but it can miss tone, idioms and context. Ask a fluent speaker to review legal, medical or official text.

Questions

Is this translator free?

Yes, with no sign-up or daily limit. Each run handles up to 5,000 characters.

Is my text sent to Google or another service?

No. Translation runs on your device. Only the open-source model files are downloaded.

Why is the first translation slow?

The translation model downloads once (about 115-650 MB depending on the languages) and is then cached by your browser.

Does it work offline?

After a language model has downloaded, translations for that pair keep working even with a weak connection.
