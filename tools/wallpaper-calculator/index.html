Wallpaper Roll Calculator

Measure the total width of the walls you'll paper and their height. Check the usable coverage on the roll label.

How it's calculated

Rolls = (wall area − openings) × (1 + pattern waste) ÷ coverage per roll, rounded up.

Worked example

InputValue
Total wall width (ft)40
Wall height (ft)8
Doors & windows area (sq ft)35
Coverage per roll (sq ft)28
Pattern waste %15
ResultValue
Area to cover (sq ft)285
Rolls needed12

Questions

Why so much waste?

Matching a large pattern repeat wastes paper on every strip. Plain papers need less.
