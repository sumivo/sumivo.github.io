Car Loan Calculator

Enter the car price, what you put down, your trade-in value and the loan terms to see what you'll pay each month.

How it's calculated

Amount financed = price × (1 + tax) − down payment − trade-in, then the standard amortization formula. Some places tax only the price after trade-in.
