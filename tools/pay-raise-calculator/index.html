Pay Raise Calculator (Real vs Nominal)

A 3% raise in a year of 3% inflation leaves you no better off. See your real raise.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Private: nothing is sent anywhere
  • Works on phone and computer

How it's calculated

Real raise = (1 + raise) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1.

Worked example

InputValue
Current salary55,000
Raise %4
Inflation %3
