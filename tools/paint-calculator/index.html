Paint Calculator (How Much Paint Do I Need?)

Measure the room, count doors and windows, and choose the number of coats. Check the coverage on your paint can.

How it's calculated

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height − 20 sq ft per door − 15 sq ft per window. Gallons = area × coats ÷ coverage.

Questions

Do I need primer?

Usually for bare plaster, big colour changes or stained walls. Primer has its own coverage rate.
