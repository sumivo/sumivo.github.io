Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Growth Calculator

For subscriptions and memberships, growth minus churn is what compounds.

How it's calculated

Future MRR = MRR × (1 + growth − churn)^months.

Worked example

InputValue
Current MRR5,000
New revenue growth % / month8
Revenue churn % / month3
Months ahead12
ResultValue

Questions

Why does churn matter so much?

Churn is subtracted every month, so reducing it compounds just like growth.
