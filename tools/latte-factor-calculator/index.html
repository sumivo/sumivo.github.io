Daily Habit Cost Calculator (Latte Factor)

Coffee, snacks, rideshares. Small daily spends add up. This isn't about guilt; it's about choosing on purpose.

How it's calculated

Monthly cost = cost × times per week × 52 ÷ 12; invested value uses monthly compounding.
