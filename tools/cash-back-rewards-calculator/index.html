Cash Back Credit Card Rewards Calculator

A card with a fee can be worth it, or not. Enter your monthly spending and the card's reward rates.

How it's calculated

Rewards = 12 × Σ(monthly spend × rate). Net = rewards − annual fee.

Worked example

InputValue
Groceries / month600
Groceries reward %3
Fuel / month200

Other reward %1
