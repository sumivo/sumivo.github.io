Inflation Calculator (Future Cost & Buying Power)

Inflation quietly raises prices every year. Enter an amount, a rate and a number of years.

How it's calculated

Future cost = A × (1 + i)^t. Buying power = A ÷ (1 + i)^t.

Worked example

InputValue
Amount today100
Inflation % per year3
Years10
ResultValue
Future cost$134.39
Buying power of today's amount$74.41
Total price increase34.4%

Questions

What rate should I use?

Long-run consumer inflation in many countries has averaged 2–4% a year, but it varies. Use a rate that fits your country.
