True Cost of an Employee Calculator

An employee costs more than their salary. Use your own rates for payroll tax and benefits.

How it's calculated

Total = salary × (1 + payroll tax) + benefits + overhead. Hourly uses hours actually worked (after holidays).

Worked example

Employer payroll taxes %7.65

Working hours per year1,880
ResultValue
