Pomodoro Session Planner

Enter how much focused work a task needs and your session lengths to plan the day realistically.

How it's calculated

Sessions = focused minutes ÷ session length (rounded up); a long break replaces every cycle-th short break.

Worked example

InputValue
Focused work needed (hours)6
Focus session (minutes)25
Short break (minutes)5
Long break (minutes)15
Sessions before a long break4
ResultValue
Focus sessions15
Breaks (short + long)14
Total time with breaks7.9 hours

Questions

Can I change the 25 minutes?

Yes. Many people work better with 45 or 50 minute sessions for deep work.
