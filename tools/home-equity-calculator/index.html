Home Equity & HELOC Calculator

Equity is your home's value minus what you owe. Lenders typically lend up to a combined loan-to-value limit.

How it's calculated

Borrowing limit = home value × max LTV − mortgage balance.

Worked example

InputValue
Home value450,000
Mortgage balance280,000
Lender's max loan-to-value %80
