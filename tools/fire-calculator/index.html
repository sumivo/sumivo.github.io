FIRE Number Calculator (Financial Independence)

Your FIRE number is the amount that could cover your yearly spending from investment returns. Find yours and your timeline.

How it's calculated

FIRE number = yearly spending ÷ withdrawal rate. Years n solve P(1 + g)^n + S((1 + g)^n − 1) ÷ g = target.

Worked example

Yearly spending40,000
Safe withdrawal rate %4
Invested now50,000

Real return % (after inflation)5

Questions

Why use a real return?

Using a return after inflation keeps everything in today's money, so the FIRE number stays meaningful.
