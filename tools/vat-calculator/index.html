VAT & Sales Tax Calculator (Add or Remove)

Choose whether your amount already includes tax. The calculator splits it into net, tax and gross.

How it's calculated

Removing tax divides by (1 + rate); adding tax multiplies by (1 + rate). Removing 20% VAT is not the same as taking 20% off.

Questions

Why isn't the tax on 120 at 20% equal to 24?

Because 120 already includes tax: the net is 100, so the tax is 20.
