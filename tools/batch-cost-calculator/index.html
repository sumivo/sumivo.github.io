Batch Production Cost per Unit Calculator

Makers often buy materials in bulk and produce in batches. Find the true cost per finished item.

How it's calculated

Cost per unit = (materials + labor hours × rate) ÷ units + packaging per unit.

Worked example

InputValue
Materials for the batch64
Units the batch makes24
Labor hours for the batch3
Hourly labor rate20
Packaging per unit0.5
ResultValue

Batch total cost$136.00
Labor share of cost44.1%
