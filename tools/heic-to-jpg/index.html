HEIC to JPG Converter (iPhone Photos)

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many Windows PCs, websites and forms won't accept. Convert them to JPG (or PNG) here: choose several photos at once, press Convert and download them individually or as a ZIP. Your photos are converted on your device, never uploaded.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose your .heic photos or drag them onto the box.
  2. Keep JPG selected (or pick PNG) and choose the quality.
  3. Press Convert, then download each photo or all of them as a ZIP.

Why HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) stores iPhone photos in about half the space of JPG, but support outside Apple devices is patchy. Converting to JPG makes photos open everywhere, from Windows Photos to government and job application forms.

How it works

The open-source libheif decoder (via the heic-to library, LGPL) runs in your browser to read HEIC files, and your browser saves them as JPG. On Safari, HEIC is read natively.

Stop new photos being saved as HEIC

On iPhone open Settings › Camera › Formats and choose “Most Compatible”. New photos are then saved as JPG.

Questions

Is quality lost when converting HEIC to JPG?

Very little at the default “High (90%)” quality; choose “Maximum” for the best possible result.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes. Choose them all; they download as a ZIP.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser.

Does it keep the photo's orientation?

Yes. Photos are turned the right way up automatically.
