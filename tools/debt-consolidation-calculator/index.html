Debt Consolidation Calculator

Enter up to three debts with their payments, then a consolidation loan's rate and term to see whether combining them saves money.

How it's calculated

Each current debt's payoff time uses n = −ln(1 − rB/P) ÷ ln(1 + r); the consolidation loan uses the amortization formula on the combined balance.
