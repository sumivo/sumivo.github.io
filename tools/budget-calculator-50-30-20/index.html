Questions

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple guideline that puts 50% of after-tax income toward needs, 30% toward wants and 20% toward savings and debt repayment.

What if my needs cost more than 50%?

That is common in high-cost areas. Change the percentages; the important part is deciding on purpose where every dollar goes.
