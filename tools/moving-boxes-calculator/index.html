Moving Boxes Estimator

A rule-of-thumb starting point. Adjust it if you're a minimalist or a collector.

How it's calculated

Boxes ≈ (15 + 20 per bedroom + 10 per person) × amount of stuff, split 40/40/20 small/medium/large.

Questions

Why so many small boxes?

Heavy items like books belong in small boxes, so they stay liftable.
