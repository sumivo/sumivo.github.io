Discount & Sale Price Calculator

"25% off, plus an extra 10% at checkout" is not 35% off. See the real final price.

How it's calculated

Final = price × (1 − d1) × (1 − d2) × (1 + tax). Stacked discounts multiply; they don't add.

Worked example

InputValue
Original price120
Discount %25
Extra discount %10
Sales tax %8
ResultValue

Real combined discount32.5%

Questions

Why isn't 25% + 10% equal to 35%?

The second discount applies to the already-reduced price, so the combined discount is 32.5%.
