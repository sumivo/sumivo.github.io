Appliance Electricity Cost Calculator

Find the wattage on the appliance label and the price per kWh on your electricity bill.

How it's calculated

kWh = watts × hours ÷ 1,000; cost = kWh × price per kWh.

Worked example

InputValue

Hours used per day3

Cost per year$263

Questions

Is the label wattage exact?

It is usually the maximum. Thermostat appliances such as heaters often draw less on average.
