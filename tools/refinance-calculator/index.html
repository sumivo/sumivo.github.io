Mortgage Refinance Calculator

Refinancing can lower your payment, but closing costs and a reset term matter. Find your break-even point.

How it's calculated

Both payments use the amortization formula. Break-even = closing costs ÷ monthly savings.

Worked example

InputValue
Current balance250,000
Current rate %7
Years left27

Closing costs5,000
