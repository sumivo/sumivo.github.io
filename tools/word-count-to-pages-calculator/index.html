Word Count to Pages Converter

Assignments often give a page count while editors count words. Convert between them quickly.

How it's calculated

About 500 words per single-spaced page in a 12 pt font, 375 at 1.5 spacing and 250 double-spaced.

Worked example

Font size12 pt (standard)

Pages double-spaced8.0

Questions

Why is it approximate?

Fonts, margins, headings and paragraph length all change how many words fit on a page.
