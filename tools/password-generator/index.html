Password Generator (Strong & Random)

Create strong, unique passwords for every account. Choose the length and character types and copy one of the generated passwords. They are made with your browser's cryptographic random number generator and never leave your device.

How to create a strong password

  1. Choose a length - 16 characters or more is recommended.
  2. Choose the character types and whether to avoid look-alike characters.
  3. Copy one of the five generated passwords, or press Generate for new ones.
  4. Save it in a password manager.

Truly random

Passwords are generated with crypto.getRandomValues, the same secure randomness used for encryption, with unbiased selection and at least one character from each type you choose.

How strong is strong?

Strength is shown in bits of entropy and the time an attacker guessing 10 billion passwords a second would need. Aim for at least 80 bits, for example 14+ characters with all character types.

Questions

Are the generated passwords stored or sent anywhere?

No. They are created in your browser and disappear when you close the page.

How long should my password be?

At least 12 characters, and 16 or more for important accounts such as email and banking.

Is it safe to test my real password here?

The check runs entirely in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, but it's good practice not to type important passwords into any website you don't need to.
