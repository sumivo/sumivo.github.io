Debt-to-Income Ratio Calculator

Lenders compare your monthly debt payments with your gross monthly income. Many look for 36% or less in total.

How it's calculated

DTI = total monthly debt payments ÷ gross monthly income.

Questions

What counts as debt?

Recurring required payments such as loans, card minimums and housing. Everyday bills like groceries and utilities usually don't count.
