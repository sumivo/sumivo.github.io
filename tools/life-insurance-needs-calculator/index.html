Life Insurance Needs Calculator

This uses a simple needs approach. Add up what your family would need, then subtract what you already have.

How it's calculated

Need = income × years + debts + mortgage + future costs − savings − existing cover.

Worked example

InputValue
Yearly income to replace60,000
Years to replace it10
Debts to clear20,000
Mortgage to clear250,000
Future costs (education, etc.)50,000
Savings & investments40,000
Existing life cover100,000
ResultValue
Suggested extra cover$780,000
Total need before savings$920,000
