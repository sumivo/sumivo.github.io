Concrete Slab Calculator (Bags & Cubic Yards)

For pads, paths and small slabs. Bag yields vary by brand, so check the bag.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Private: nothing is sent anywhere
  • Works on phone and computer

How it's calculated

Volume = length × width × thickness ÷ 12 × (1 + waste); bags = volume ÷ bag yield, rounded up.

Questions

When should I order ready-mix instead?

Above about one cubic yard, a ready-mix delivery is often easier and cheaper than mixing bags.
