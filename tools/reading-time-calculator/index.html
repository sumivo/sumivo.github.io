Reading Time & Speaking Time Calculator

Useful for blog posts, speeches, presentations and study material.

How it's calculated

Time = word count ÷ words per minute. Adults commonly read around 200–260 words per minute.

Worked example

InputValue
Word count2,500
Reading speed (words per minute)238
Speaking speed (words per minute)130
ResultValue
Reading time (minutes)10.5
Speaking time (minutes)19.23
Pages (≈300 words each)8.33
