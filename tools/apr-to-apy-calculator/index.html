APR to APY Calculator

APY includes the effect of compounding, so it is the fairer way to compare savings accounts and loans.

How it's calculated

APY = (1 + APR ÷ k)^k − 1, where k is the number of compounding periods per year.

Questions

Which should I compare, APR or APY?

For savings, compare APY. For loans, lenders usually quote APR; convert both to APY to compare like with like.
