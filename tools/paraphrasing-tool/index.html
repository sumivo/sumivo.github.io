Paraphrasing Tool (Free AI Rewriter)

Rephrase sentences, paragraphs and essays with an AI model that runs on your own device. Changed words are highlighted, and you can click any sentence for alternatives. There is no account, no daily limit and no server: your text never leaves your browser.

How to paraphrase text

  1. Paste or type your text into the left box.
  2. Choose an engine. Fast works everywhere; Better AI adds modes such as Formal, Simple, Shorten and Expand on computers with a modern graphics card.
  3. Set the creativity and, for Better AI, the mode and any words to keep unchanged.
  4. Press Paraphrase, then click any sentence for alternative versions and copy the result.

Two engines, both private

The Fast engine uses an open-source T5 paraphrase model that runs in any modern browser, including phones; it downloads about 250 MB the first time and is cached after that. The Better AI engine uses Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, an open-source language model, through WebLLM on your graphics card; it downloads about 1 GB once and understands instructions such as tone and length.

Modes

Standard rewords naturally. Fluency fixes grammar and flow with minimal changes. Formal suits business and academic writing. Simple uses plain words and short sentences. Creative changes more. Shorten removes about a third of the words, and Expand adds detail without inventing facts.

Use it responsibly

Paraphrasing is great for clearer emails, simpler explanations and fresh wording of your own drafts. Rewording someone else's work does not make it yours, so cite your sources, and always check that the rewritten text still says what you mean, because AI can occasionally change a meaning or a number.

Questions

Is this paraphrasing tool free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no daily word limit. Each run handles up to 600 words with Fast and 1,500 with Better AI, and you can run it as often as you like.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Both AI models run inside your browser on your own device. Only the model files are downloaded, once, from Hugging Face.

Why does the first run take a while?

The AI model has to download once (about 250 MB for Fast, about 1 GB for Better AI). After that it is stored by your browser and starts much faster.

Why is Better AI unavailable on my device?

It needs WebGPU, which is available in recent Chrome and Edge on most computers. Phones and older browsers can use the Fast engine instead.

Can I keep certain words unchanged?

Yes. With Better AI, list them in “Freeze words”, for example names or technical terms.

Will the result pass plagiarism or AI detectors?

We don't claim that. The tool is for improving wording, not for hiding where text came from. Quote and cite sources properly.
