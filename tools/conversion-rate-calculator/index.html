Conversion Rate & Revenue per Visitor Calculator

Revenue per visitor combines conversion rate and order value, so it is a single number to improve.

How it's calculated

Conversion rate = orders ÷ visitors. Revenue per visitor = revenue ÷ visitors.

What is a typical conversion rate?

Online shops often see 1–3%, but it varies widely by product, price and traffic source.
