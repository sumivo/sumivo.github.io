CAGR Calculator (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

CAGR smooths growth into one yearly rate, so investments and businesses can be compared fairly.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Private: nothing is sent anywhere
  • Works on phone and computer

How it's calculated

CAGR = (end ÷ start)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

Worked example

InputValue
Starting value10,000
Ending value18,000
Years5
ResultValue
CAGR12.47%
Total growth80.0%

Questions

Is CAGR the same as average return?

No. An average of yearly returns overstates growth when returns vary; CAGR gives the actual compounded rate.
