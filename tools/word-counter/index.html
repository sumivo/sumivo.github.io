Word Counter (Words, Characters & Reading Time)

Check the length of essays, assignments, posts and web copy as you type. See words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, reading and speaking time, your most-used words and how your text fits common length limits.

How to count words

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. The counts update instantly as you write.
  3. Use the length limits and keyword table to trim or improve your text.

More than a word count

Reading time uses 238 words a minute and speaking time 150, typical adult averages. The keyword table shows your most frequent meaningful words and their density, which helps avoid repetition and check SEO copy.

Private

Your text is counted in your browser and never stored or sent anywhere, so it's safe for unpublished work.

Questions

Does it count words in Hindi and other languages?

Yes. Words are counted in any language that separates words with spaces, including Hindi and other Indian languages.

Are spaces counted as characters?

Both are shown - characters with spaces and without spaces.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page.
