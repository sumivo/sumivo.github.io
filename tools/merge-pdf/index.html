Merge PDF (Combine PDF Files)

Join PDFs into a single document: add the files, put them in the order you want and press Merge. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text stays sharp and searchable. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose two or more PDF files, or drag them onto the box.
  2. Put them in order with the arrows.
  3. Press Merge. The combined PDF downloads straight away.

Exact copies of your pages

Pages are copied, not re-rendered, so fonts, links-free layout and image quality are unchanged and the file size is roughly the sum of the originals. Useful for application forms, invoices, reports and scanned documents.

Private

The open-source pdf-lib library combines the files on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it is safe for bank statements, ID documents and contracts.

Questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

As many as your device's memory allows; dozens of normal documents are fine.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first (open the PDF with the password and save or print it as a new PDF), then merge.

Does merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up, watermark or limits.
