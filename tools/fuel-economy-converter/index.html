Fuel Economy Converter (MPG to L/100km)

Car reviews from different countries quote fuel economy in different units. Convert in one step.

How it's calculated

L/100km = 235.215 ÷ US mpg. One US gallon is 3.785 litres; a UK gallon is 4.546 litres.

Questions

Why is lower better for L/100km?

It measures fuel used per distance, so fewer litres means better economy.
