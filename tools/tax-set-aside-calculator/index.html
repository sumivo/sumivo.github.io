Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator

Avoid a tax-time surprise. Use your own estimated total tax rate on profit.

How it's calculated

Set-aside = (revenue − expenses) × estimated tax rate. A planning estimate, not tax advice.

What rate should I use?

Combine income tax and any self-employment or social taxes. An accountant can give you a reliable figure.
