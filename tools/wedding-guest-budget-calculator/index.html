Wedding Guest List Budget Calculator

Some wedding costs are fixed (venue, attire); others grow with every guest (food, drinks, favours). Split them to size your list.

How it's calculated

Guests = (budget − fixed costs) ÷ cost per guest, rounded down.

Worked example

InputValue
Total budget25,000
Fixed costs9,000
Cost per guest110

Questions

What counts as per-guest cost?

Catering, drinks, cake, chairs and favours. Anything that scales with headcount.
