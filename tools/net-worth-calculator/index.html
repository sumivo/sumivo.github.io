Net Worth Calculator

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. Tracking it once a quarter shows real progress.

How it's calculated

Net worth = total assets − total liabilities.

Worked example

InputValue
Cash & savings8,000
Investments15,000
Retirement accounts42,000
Home value0
Vehicles12,000
Mortgage balance0
Car & student loans18,000
Credit cards & other debt2,500
ResultValue
Net worth$56,500
Total assets$77,000
Total debts$20,500

Questions

Is a negative net worth bad?

It is common early in a career, especially with student loans. The trend over time matters more than the number.
