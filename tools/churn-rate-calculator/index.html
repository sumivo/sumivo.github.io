Customer Churn & Retention Calculator

Churn is the share of customers who leave in a period. Average lifetime is roughly 1 ÷ churn.

How it's calculated

Churn = customers lost ÷ customers at start. Average lifetime ≈ 1 ÷ monthly churn.

Worked example

Customers at start of month400
Customers lost18

Questions

Should new customers count?

Not in the churn rate; measure churn on the customers you had at the start.
