Credit Card Payoff Calculator

Enter your balance, APR and what you can pay each month. The calculator shows when the card is cleared and the payment needed to clear it in a year.

How it's calculated

Months n = −ln(1 − r × B ÷ P) ÷ ln(1 + r), with balance B, payment P and monthly rate r = APR ÷ 12.
