Compress PDF (Reduce PDF Size)

Make a PDF small enough to email or upload to a form with a size limit. The recommended method recompresses only the pictures inside the PDF, so text stays sharp, selectable and searchable and links and forms keep working. For scanned documents, the Maximum method turns pages into compressed images for the smallest possible file.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Files stay on your device
  • Works on phone and computer

How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF or drag it onto the box.
  2. Keep “Keep text - compress images only”, or choose Maximum for scans.
  3. Pick a compression level, and black and white if colour isn't needed.
  4. Press Compress PDF and download the smaller file.

Two methods

Keep text finds every large picture inside the PDF (photos, scanned pages, screenshots), resizes it to a sensible resolution and re-saves it as a compressed JPEG. Everything else - text, fonts, links, bookmarks and form fields - is left exactly as it was. Maximum redraws every page as a compressed image, which gives the biggest savings for scans but makes text unselectable.

When it won't help

PDFs that are mostly typed text are usually small already, and some image types (CMYK print images, special colour spaces) are left alone to avoid colour changes. If the result isn't smaller, the tool tells you and keeps your original.

Questions

Will the text still be selectable after compressing?

Yes with the recommended Keep text method. Only the Maximum method turns pages into images.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

PDFs with photos or scans typically shrink by 40-90%. Text-only PDFs may not shrink at all.

How do I get a PDF under a size limit like 1 MB or 200 KB?

Start with Recommended. If it's still too big, choose Strong, tick black and white, or use Maximum for scanned documents.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser.
