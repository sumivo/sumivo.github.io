ROAS Calculator (Return on Ad Spend)

A 3x ROAS can still lose money if your margins are thin. Compare it with your break-even ROAS.

How it's calculated

ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin.

Worked example

Revenue from ads4,200
Gross margin %35

Questions

Is ROAS the same as ROI?

No. ROAS uses revenue; ROI uses profit. A campaign can have good ROAS and negative ROI.
