College Savings Calculator

Tuition tends to rise faster than general prices. Plan with an inflation rate for education costs.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Private: nothing is sent anywhere
  • Works on phone and computer

How it's calculated

Future cost = yearly cost × years × (1 + inflation)^years until college (simplified). Monthly saving = shortfall × r ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1).

Worked example

Questions

Why is the total an estimate?

Costs are inflated to the first year of college only; later years would cost a little more.
