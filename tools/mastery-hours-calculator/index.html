10,000 Hours Mastery Calculator

The 10,000-hour idea is a rough benchmark, not a law, but it is a useful way to see what consistent practice adds up to.

How it's calculated

Years = remaining hours ÷ (hours per day × days per week × 52).

Worked example

InputValue
Hours goal10,000
Hours already practised500
Hours per practice day2
Practice days per week5
ResultValue

Hours per year at this pace520
Years to reach the goal18.3 years
