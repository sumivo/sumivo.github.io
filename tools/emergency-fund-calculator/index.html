Emergency Fund Calculator

A common target is three to six months of essential expenses. Work out your number and your timeline.

How it's calculated

Target = essential monthly expenses × months of cover. Time = remaining gap ÷ monthly savings.

Worked example

Essential expenses / month2,500
Months of cover6
Saved so far3,000

Questions

How many months should I choose?

Three months is a common minimum; six or more makes sense with irregular income or dependants.
