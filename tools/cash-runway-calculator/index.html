Cash Runway & Burn Rate Calculator

Runway is how long you can operate before the money runs out at today's spending.

How it's calculated

Runway = cash ÷ (monthly expenses − monthly revenue).

Worked example

InputValue
Cash in the bank60,000
Monthly revenue8,000
Monthly expenses14,000
ResultValue
Net burn per month$6,000.00
Runway10 months
Revenue needed to break even$14,000.00
