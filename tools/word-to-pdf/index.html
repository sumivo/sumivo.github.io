Word to PDF Converter (DOCX & DOC)

Convert a Word document to PDF without installing anything or uploading it. Choose a .docx file to keep its fonts, images, tables, headers and footers, or an older .doc file to convert its text. You see a preview first, then save it as a PDF with your browser's built-in PDF printer.

How to convert Word to PDF

  1. Choose a .docx, .doc or .txt file, or drag it onto the box.
  2. Check the preview. For .doc and .txt files, pick A4 or US Letter.
  3. Press Download PDF.
  4. In the print window, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer and press Save.

Why it uses “Save as PDF”

Your browser already contains an excellent PDF writer. Using it keeps the text selectable and searchable, embeds the right fonts for every language (including Hindi, Arabic, Chinese and more), and means your document never has to be uploaded to a conversion server.

DOCX and DOC files

Modern .docx files are laid out page by page with the open-source docx-preview library, keeping headings, fonts, colours, tables, images, lists, headers and footers. Old Word 97–2003 .doc files use a closed binary format that browsers cannot lay out faithfully, so they are converted as clean text with paragraphs and table rows kept. For an exact copy of a .doc file, open it in Word or Google Docs, save it as .docx and convert that.

On a phone

On Android, choose “Save as PDF” in the print window. On iPhone, tap Print, then pinch outwards on the preview (or tap Share) to save the PDF to Files.

Questions

Is my Word document uploaded anywhere?

No. The document is read and laid out in your browser, and your browser writes the PDF. Nothing is sent to a server.

Will the PDF look exactly like my Word document?

For .docx files, very close. Most documents (text, headings, tables, images, headers and footers) look the same. Unusual features such as text boxes, charts or SmartArt may shift slightly, which is why you see a preview first.

Can I convert an old .doc file?

Yes, as text. Paragraphs and table rows are kept, but fonts, images and table borders are not. Save the file as .docx in Word or Google Docs for a full-fidelity PDF.

Why does a print window open?

The browser's “Save as PDF” printer creates a high-quality PDF with selectable text and correct fonts for any language. Choose “Save as PDF” as the destination instead of a real printer.

Is there a file size or page limit?

No fixed limit. Very long documents (hundreds of pages) may take a little longer to lay out on older phones.
