Solar Panel Payback Calculator

Rising electricity prices shorten the payback. Enter your own quote and expected savings.

How it's calculated

Payback years n solve savings × ((1 + g)^n − 1) ÷ g = net cost, with yearly savings growing by g.

Worked example

InputValue
System cost18,000
Incentives / tax credit %30
Bill savings per month120
Electricity price rise % per year3
ResultValue

Payback period7.9 years
Net savings over 25 years$39,901

Questions

What else affects payback?

Panel degradation, maintenance, financing costs and how much power you use yourself versus export.
