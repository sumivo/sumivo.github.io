Dimensional Weight Calculator for Shipping

Carriers charge by size or weight, whichever is greater. Check your carrier's divisor and units.

How it's calculated

Dimensional weight = length × width × height ÷ divisor (for example 139 for inches and pounds).

Worked example

InputValue
Length16
Width12
Height10
Carrier divisor139
Actual weight8
ResultValue
