Rule of 72 Calculator

Divide 72 by the yearly return to estimate the doubling time. Here is the shortcut and the exact figure side by side.

How it's calculated

Doubling time ≈ 72 ÷ rate. Exact time = ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + rate).

Questions

When is the rule less accurate?

It works best for rates between about 6% and 10%; for very high rates the error grows.
