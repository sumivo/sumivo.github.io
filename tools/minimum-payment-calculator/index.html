Credit Card Minimum Payment Calculator

Card minimums are usually a small percentage of the balance with a fixed floor. Paying only that can take decades. See the real cost.

How it's calculated

While the percentage applies, the balance shrinks by a factor (1 + r)(1 − p) each month; once the payment hits the floor, it is paid off like a fixed payment.
