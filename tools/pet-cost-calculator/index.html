Pet Cost Calculator (Yearly & Lifetime)

Plan for the full commitment before bringing a pet home, or budget better for the one you have.

How it's calculated

Yearly = (monthly costs × 12) + yearly costs; lifetime = yearly × lifespan.

Worked example

InputValue
Food per month50
Insurance per month30
Vet care per year400
Grooming per year200
Toys, litter & supplies per year150
Expected lifespan (years)12
ResultValue
Cost per year$1,710
Cost per month$142.50
Lifetime cost$20,520

Questions

What about emergencies?

Keep a pet emergency fund or insurance; a single emergency visit can cost more than a year of routine care.
