Straight-Line Depreciation Calculator

Straight-line depreciation spreads an asset's cost evenly over its useful life.

How it's calculated

Yearly depreciation = (cost − salvage value) ÷ useful life.

Worked example

InputValue
Asset cost12,000
Salvage value2,000
Useful life (years)5
Years in use2
ResultValue
