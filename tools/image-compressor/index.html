Image Compressor (Compress to KB)

Shrink photos for email, websites and online application forms. Compress by quality, or set a target size such as 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB and the tool finds the best quality that fits. Batch compress many images at once, right in your browser.

How to compress an image to a specific size

  1. Choose your images or drag them onto the box.
  2. Choose “To a target size” and enter the size in KB (for example 50), or choose “By quality”.
  3. Optionally limit the dimensions, which helps reach very small sizes.
  4. Press Compress and download.

Built for form limits

Exam, job and government application forms often require photos under 20-100 KB and signatures under 10-20 KB. The target-size mode searches for the highest quality that fits, and shrinks the dimensions only if quality alone isn't enough.

Smart format choice

Automatic mode saves photos as JPG and images with transparency as WebP. PNG is lossless, so it can only get smaller by resizing.

Questions

How do I compress a photo to under 50 KB?

Choose “To a target size”, enter 50 and press Compress. If the photo is large, also choose a maximum size such as 800 px.

Will compression reduce quality?

Slightly. At the Recommended setting the difference is hard to see while files are usually 60-80% smaller.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your device.
