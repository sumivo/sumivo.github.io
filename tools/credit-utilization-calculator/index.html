Credit Utilization Calculator

Utilization (balances ÷ limits) is a major credit score factor. Lower is generally better.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Private: nothing is sent anywhere
  • Works on phone and computer

How it's calculated

Utilization = total balances ÷ total credit limits.

Questions

Does closing a card help?

Usually not. It lowers your total limit, which can raise utilization.
