Privacy

Calculators: everything you type into our calculators stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

PDF, image, text, speech and translation tools: your files, photos and text are processed on your own device, inside your browser. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we don't store them.

AI model downloads: the paraphraser, background remover, translator and speech-to-text tools download open-source AI models (not your data) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time you use them; the Better AI paraphraser also downloads a small program file from GitHub, and image to text downloads language data from jsDelivr. Where your browser has a built-in on-device translator, the translator uses that instead. Like any download, those services see your IP address. Your browser then keeps the models in its cache so later uses are faster; you can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Text to speech: reading aloud uses the voices of your device and browser. Voices the tool marks as “online” (such as Microsoft Edge's Natural voices) are provided by your browser's maker over the internet, so the text being read is sent to that company. Choose a voice without the “online” label to keep the text on your device.

Web searches: the plagiarism checker's “search the web” links open Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo with the sentence you chose, under that search engine's own privacy policy. Nothing is searched unless you click a link.

Analytics: we don't use analytics or tracking cookies.

Purchases: checkout is handled by our store providers (Gumroad for US dollars, Instamojo for Indian rupees, or Ko-fi), which process your payment and email under their own privacy policies. We receive your email address and order details in order to deliver your files and provide support.

Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

AI model terms: the Fast paraphraser uses a model released under the OpenRAIL licence, which forbids harmful uses such as generating disinformation, harassment or content that breaks the law. By using the paraphraser you agree not to use it for those purposes.
